# Classic Plus — Rules Reference

Source: https://www.fantacalcio.it/regolamenti/classic-plus

## Overview

Classic Plus is a new game mode sitting alongside Classic and Mantra. For
the 2025/26 season it's exclusive to the **FantaChampions** app, not the
general Leghe Fantacalcio platform (yet).

The name is deliberate: despite first impressions, **it is explicitly not a
hybrid with Mantra** — fantacalcio.it frames it as an evolution of *Classic*
specifically.

## Motivation

Classic's structure (formations, player roles) only has three lines —
defense/midfield/attack — with no separate trequarti (attacking-midfield)
line, even though today's football is dominated globally by 4-2-3-1-style
shapes (with 3-4-2-1 as the well-known Italian alternative). Classic Plus
adds that fourth line, deliberately gently, while keeping Classic's
simplicity intact.

## Roles (6 categories, single-role like Classic)

Players stay **single-role** (monoruolo), like Classic — but the categories
themselves are redrawn in more modern terms than Classic's plain P/D/C/A.
One thing *is* borrowed from Mantra: **no fixed per-role roster quotas** —
the manager assembles the squad freely around their own tactical plan
rather than filling fixed D/C/A slot counts.

| Code | Category |
|---|---|
| **P** | Portieri — Goalkeepers |
| **D** | Difensori centrali e braccetti — Central defenders and wide/hybrid center-backs (the "braccetto" wide-CB role from a back-3, same concept as Mantra's B) |
| **E** | Terzini e quinti — Fullbacks and wing-backs |
| **C** | Mediani, registi e mezzali — Defensive midfielders, deep playmakers, and box-to-box midfielders |
| **F** | Trequartisti, ali ed attaccanti esterni — Attacking midfielders, wingers, and wide forwards |
| **A** | Prime e seconde punte — Out-and-out and support strikers |

## Formations (3 available)

The graphic on the page shows three pitch diagrams without individual
labels — described here by their actual role layout rather than a
traditional formation-number guess:

1. **3 D + 2 E (wing-backs) — 2 C + 1 hybrid C/F — 1 hybrid F/A — 1 A**:
   a back-3 with wing-backs, roughly a 3-4-2-1/3-4-1-2 shape with two
   flexible slots that can swing between midfield and attack.
2. **2 E + 2 D (back-4) — 3 C — 1 F + 1 hybrid F/A — 1 A**: a back-4 with
   three central midfielders, then an F plus an F/A hybrid supporting a
   lone striker.
3. **2 E + 2 D (back-4) — 2 C — 3 F — 1 A**: a back-4, double pivot, and
   three F's across the attacking-midfield line supporting a lone striker —
   this is the one matching the 4-2-3-1 shape referenced as globally
   dominant.

Where a slot shows two role letters together (e.g. "C/F"), that's a
flexible position — same alternative-role concept as Mantra's dual-role
formation slots.

## FantaChampions Implementation (current, 2025/26)

- A **fixed +0.5 modifier** applies to the back-3 formation (option 1
  above), to balance it against the two back-4 options.
- More broadly, Classic Plus on FantaChampions is a **locked/standardized**
  ruleset — consistent with running inside a contest, not
  league-customizable there.

## Planned for Leghe Fantacalcio (future)

Once it rolls into the general private-leagues platform, Classic Plus is
expected to get its own customizable kit: substitution modes, modifiers,
and dedicated options — the same configurability model Classic/Mantra
already have.
