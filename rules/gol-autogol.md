# Goal & Own-Goal Attribution — Rules Reference

Source: https://www.fantacalcio.it/regolamenti/gol-autogol

## Overview

Rules for who gets credited with a goal in disputed situations, following
FIFA/UEFA criteria. **Lega Serie A's official determination is binding** —
it overrides live broadcast/match data when the two disagree.

- Disputed goals are reviewed the day after each matchday, with decisions
  published on legaseriea.it; fantacalcio.it's own corrections follow suit
  on its site and social channels.
- Worth remembering: Lega Serie A's ruling isn't necessarily the same as
  what the live match report ("tabellino") showed during or right after the
  game — that's just a simple livescore feed, not the final word.
- Default tiebreak when it's genuinely unclear whether a shot was on target:
  the shooter gets the credit.

## What Counts as an Own Goal

**Defensive errors (own action goes wrong):**
- A misdirected back-pass, a botched defensive clearance, or a *deliberate*
  attempt to sweep/clear the ball away that goes wrong — note this is about
  the defender's own clearance action backfiring, not about deflecting an
  opponent's effort (that's the separate case below).

**Unintentional deflections:**
- A defender redirects an opponent's shot, cross, or pass into their own net,
  when that original play wasn't already headed for goal.

## What Does NOT Count as an Own Goal

- An instinctive/reflexive block of a shot that was already on target
  (credited to the original shooter) — "instinctive" means interposing a
  body part without actively trying to kick the ball, including something
  as simple as spreading a leg "wall-style" to protect the goal.
- A clearance attempt when the goalkeeper is clearly already beaten.
- Exception: if the shot hits the woodwork (post/crossbar) and *then* gets
  deflected in by a defending player — including the goalkeeper — that
  does count as an own goal.

## Teammate Deflections

- **Shot already on target**: an unintentional deflection keeps the credit
  with the original shooter; an active/deliberate deflection that changes the
  trajectory transfers credit to the teammate who touched it.
- **Shot not originally on target**: if a teammate's deflection sends it in,
  credit goes to whoever had the final touch.
- When it's unclear where the original shot was headed, credit defaults to
  the original shooter.
